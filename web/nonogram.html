Nonogram

How to play

The numbers say how many squares in a row (or column) are filled, in order, with at least one gap between each group. Fill them all and a picture appears.
×Tap once for ×, twice to fill, a third time clears. The switch at the top picks what a tap and a drag do: ×, ■ fill, or Clear. Drag along a row or column to do many at once.
Tap a row's or column's numbers: they come up big at the bottom. Tap a number to slash it off as you place it. Slash the last one and × goes in every empty square of that line. Swipe the bar down (or tap the numbers again) to close it.
On 15×15, pinch with two fingers to zoom, and slide two fingers to move. One finger always plays. Fit shows the whole board again.
Stuck? Hint checks for a wrong square first, then shows one row or column you can work out, and why.

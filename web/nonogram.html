Nonogram

How to play

The numbers say how many squares in a row (or column) are filled, in order, with at least one gap between each group. Fill them all and a picture appears.
×Tap once for ×, twice to fill, a third time clears. Pick ■ at the top to fill first. Drag along a row or column to do many: it paints what your first square shows, so to fill a run, fill the first square, then drag from it.
Filled a line right? Tap its numbers and × goes in every square left. If something's off, the numbers just give a little shake.
On 15×15, pinch with two fingers to zoom, and slide two fingers to move. One finger always plays. Fit shows the whole board again.
Stuck? Hint checks for a wrong square first, then shows one row or column you can work out, and why.

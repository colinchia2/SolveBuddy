Shikaku

How to play

Cut the square into rectangles. Each one holds exactly one number and covers that many squares.
Patches: each clue's tile shows its shape: a square, a tall rectangle, a wide one, or a dashed tile for any. A number on it is the size; no number, any size.
Drag from one square to another to draw a patch; its size shows as you go. Drag from a patch to make it bigger. Tap a patch to take it away.
A patch with no number, two numbers, or too many squares turns red and says why. One that's still too small has a dashed edge.
Stuck? Hint checks for a wrong patch first, then shows a number you can work out, and why. Sometimes it draws only the part that's certain; drag from it to finish.

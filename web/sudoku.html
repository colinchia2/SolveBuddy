How to play

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9, each exactly once.
Corner marks: numbers that must go in one of a few cells in a box.
Center marks: every number this one cell could still be.
Stuck? Hint checks for mistakes first, then shows the simplest next step.

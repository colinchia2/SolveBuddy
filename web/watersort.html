Water Sort

How to play

Sort the colours so every tube holds just one colour.
Tap a tube, then another, to pour its top colour across.
You can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube, and only if there's room (4 per tube).
Stuck? Hint finds a way to finish from where you are, says why that pour helps, or tells you to undo.

Controls

  • Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap where to pour. Tap it again to put it down.
  • Tap anywhere while it pours to speed it up.
  • Undo takes back a pour; Restart starts the level over.
  • Hint: Nudge, then Explain, then Show me pours it for you.

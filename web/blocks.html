How to play

Blocks fall one at a time. Fill a whole row to clear it and score.
Four rows at once scores the most. Every 10 rows is a new level, and blocks fall faster. The game ends when the blocks reach the top.

Controls

Tap anywhere to turn the piece.
Swipe left or right to move it.
Hold your finger down, or drag down, to drop faster.
Quick flick down to drop it all the way. Dropping faster scores more.
Swipe up (or tap Save) to keep a piece for later and swap it back in. Once per piece, until it lands.

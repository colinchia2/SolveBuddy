How to play

Blocks fall one at a time. Fill a whole row to clear it and score.
Tap the well to turn. Swipe left or right to move, drag down to speed up, flick down to drop.
More rows at once score more. Every 10 rows is a new level, and blocks fall faster.
The game ends when the blocks reach the top.

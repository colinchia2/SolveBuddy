How it plays

  1. Drag from any square: a patch follows your finger steadily (no wobble). Its size shows in the corner (2×4 = 8). Start a drag on a patch you already drew and it grows from there; it never starts over.
  2. Let go: a right-sized patch lands in its own colour. A drag over other patches replaces them.
  3. Wrong? The patch turns hatched red and says why ("This patch needs 8 squares. It has 6."). Nothing is ever checked against the answer, only against its own number.
  4. Tap a patch to remove it. Undo and Clear work as in the other games.
  5. Finish: the patches dip and pop in a wave from the top-left corner, fill in solid, and the grid becomes a picture.
  6. Buzzes: a tick for each new square while dragging, medium when a patch lands, warn on red, success on the finish.

Nonogram: the clue bar

The one design, from your notes on the two directions (10/24). Your Hard 15×15 #2. Mockup only, nothing is built.

  • Top switch: × / ■ / Clear. Dragging works in all three.
  • Tapping squares never changes anything at the bottom.
  • Tap a clue and a bar slides up from the bottom with just that line's numbers, big. It lies on top; nothing else moves.
  • Tap a number to slash it. The small numbers on the board can be tapped one by one too.
  • Last number slashed = the line's empty squares fill with × by themselves. No other buttons.
  • Close it: tap the same clue again or swipe it down. Tapping another clue swaps the bar to that line.
  • Clues never grey by themselves, and tapping a clue never checks your line.
